## Acknowledgment note by the external contributor
  % 28-AND circuit for the AES S-box (multiplicative complexity <= 28; previous public record: 29).
  % Three layers: level-one products of linear forms in x (spanning the norm N = x^17) | GF(2^4) inversion of N |
  % products of linear forms in (x, u) with u = N + N^-1.  AND-depth 5: 10 | 5 | 13;  AND-depth 4: 9 | 6 (AND-depth 2) | 13.
  % Design lineage: tower-field construction of Boyar-Peralta; the GF(2^4)-inversion stage follows the known 5-AND design
  % (Boyar-Peralta; also used in the 29-AND circuits of @umizame and NIST).  Linear layers optimised in {AND, XOR, XNOR}.
  % Found and verified with AI assistance: exhaustive 256-input check, ABC cec, Lean 4 kernel-checked proof of equivalence with FIPS 197.
